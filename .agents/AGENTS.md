# Workspace Rules & Styling Guidelines

## 1. Design & Layout Best Practices

* **Solid OKLCH Surfaces:** Do not replace every background with a gradient. Keep cards, tables, dialogs, and main page segments styled with solid OKLCH surfaces (e.g. `canvas.default`, `canvas.soft`, `canvas.deep`) to maintain high legibility and clean contrast. Gradients should only be used as composite design highlights or ambient indicators.
